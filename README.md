## How I think about AI products

- **Show the uncertainty.** Confidence scoring, provenance and human review beat a clean interface that quietly guesses, especially when someone signs off on the output.
- **Cost is a product decision.** Latency, inference spend and quality are the real trade-off triangle.
- **Say what was measured.** Production results, eval results and early reads are different kinds of evidence, and I label them.
- **Put AI in the workflow too.** At Taggbox I led internal AI adoption across engineering, QA, marketing and PRD tooling, while keeping decisions human.
